Nhạc

Live band ChordPro reader & stage viewer

Nhạc (Vietnamese for “music”) is a free web app for gigging musicians, worship teams and band rehearsals. It replaces paper binders and PDF zooming with a fast chord-sheet reader that fits a whole song on one screen, with no mid-song scrolling or page turns.

Open Nhạc

What it does

  • Read and edit songs in the ChordPro format, with lyrics and chords.
  • Transpose to any key and set a capo in real time.
  • Build and reorder setlists for a gig or rehearsal.
  • Stage mode: an auto-fitting, one-screen layout for tablets and music stands.
  • Works fully offline once installed as a PWA on iPad, Android or desktop.
  • Your songs live on your own device (in the browser’s IndexedDB), not on our servers.

Optional Google Drive integration

If you choose to connect Google Drive, Nhạc uses Google sign-in to:

  • Import the chord sheets you pick in Google’s file picker (drive.file, which only reaches files you pick or that the app created).
  • Publish and pull a single library file containing your songs and setlists in a folder you pick.

This is entirely optional and only happens when you click it. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
