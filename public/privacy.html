Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Nhạc (“the app”, “we”) is a free ChordPro reader and setlist manager available at this website. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: we don’t run a server that collects your data. Everything stays in your browser or in your own Google Drive.

1. Data stored on your device

Songs, setlists, settings and your optional Google Drive settings are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB and localStorage). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly use an export or Google Drive feature. Clearing your browser data removes it.

2. Google Drive (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks for your permission using Google sign-in and requests only one scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file – lets the app read only the chord-sheet files you select in Google’s file picker (so they can be imported into the app), and create, update and read a single library file (your songs and setlists) in a Drive folder you pick. This scope cannot see any other files in your Drive.

The access token is obtained in your browser and used directly between your browser and Google. The Google Client ID you enter is stored only in your browser. We do not receive, store, or have access to your Google account, your tokens, or your Drive files.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

Nhạc’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing features described above.
  • It is not transferred to anyone else, except as needed to provide those features at your request.
  • It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold.
  • No humans read your data; it is processed only in your browser.

4. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information, create accounts, or use advertising or tracking cookies. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may process standard technical request data (such as IP address) in server logs to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, under Cloudflare’s privacy policy. The app loads fonts from Google Fonts and, when you use Drive features, scripts from Google; Google may receive your IP address for those requests.

5. Revoking access

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time in the app, and revoke Nhạc’s access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the library file from your Drive removes the published copy.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and date will be posted on this page.
